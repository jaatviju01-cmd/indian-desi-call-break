How this version works
Cards fly one by one: deal 5, pause for a 13-second trump selection, then deal 4 + 4. All players bid after receiving 13 cards. Standard Call Break scoring is used: make your bid to score the bid plus 0.1 per extra trick; miss it and lose the bid amount. Offline computer opponents are included. Online multiplayer and private rooms require a connected backend and will be added in the next stage.
